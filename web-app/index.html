HIKING SURVIVAL

HOW TO PLAY

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Reach The Summit — Station 25 of 25, across 5 days of high-altitude trekking. One wrong move can end everything.

💀 FAILURE

If any of your three stats hits 0%, the expedition ends. Manage all three — don't let one silently drain away.

⚡ STAMINA · 🍖 HUNGER · 🔥 WARMTH

Every action costs stats. Moving drains all three. Resting restores Stamina & Warmth but burns Hunger fast.

🗺️ TERRAIN TYPES

  • Safe / Normal — light drain
  • Strenuous — heavy Stamina penalty
  • Danger — severe Warmth drain
  • Camp — partial recovery on arrival

🎒 ITEMS

Click any item in your backpack to use it immediately. Crampons equip permanently and reduce Stamina drain on icy terrain.

⚠️ RANDOM EVENTS

Every non-camp move triggers a random event — a boot slip, sudden windchill, or a detour. These cost extra stats on top of the base move cost.
